# Roadmap

## Done

- Core loop: pick kanji/words, run a quiz, see score, results kept on device.
- Three question types: reading, meaning, picture recognition.
- Multiple choice or typing, adjustable difficulty, hints.
- Save/reuse/reset practice presets.
- Reports page: accuracy by kanji, set and format, speed, mistakes, export/import history.
- Polishing setup and reports: clearer filters, pagination, date range picking,
  a real result-screen animation, preset bug fixes.
- Chart page: every word as a flip card, with the picture and kanji on the
  front and the reading, romaji, meaning and each kanji's on/kun readings on the
  back. Filter by level, word shape, category, subcategory or search.
- Printable flashcards: pick words, choose a 1×1 to 4×4 grid, and export a
  double-sided A4 PDF in the background with progress and cancel.
- Icons for every category and subcategory, and expand/collapse all on the
  word lists.

## Next

- Harder, exam-realistic wrong answers.
- Audio questions.
- Downloadable content packs instead of everything bundled.
- Timed runs.
- A kanji component-building quiz mode.
- Full platform support + accessibility polish.
- More JLPT levels.
- Later: on-device handwriting and speech tutoring (v2.0+).
