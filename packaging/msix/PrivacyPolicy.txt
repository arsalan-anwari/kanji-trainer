Privacy Policy for Kanji Trainer

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Kanji Trainer is a practice app for Japanese vocabulary written in kanji. This
policy explains what the app does with your data. The short version: it does
not collect any of it.

What the app collects

Nothing. Kanji Trainer does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. There
are no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no
crash reporting.

What the app stores on your device

The app saves your practice settings, the word packs you installed and the
score reports of your runs. This data stays on your computer, in the local
application data folder that Windows gives the app. It is never uploaded
anywhere.

You can export your reports to a .kj-report file and import them again, for
example to move them to another device. That file is written where you choose
to save it, and moving it somewhere else is entirely up to you.

Uninstalling the app removes it along with the data it stored.

Network use

Kanji Trainer practises offline. The only network requests it makes are
reading the list of available word packs and downloading a pack you chose to
install, both from the app's public dataset on Hugging Face
(huggingface.co/datasets/arsalan-anwari/kanji-data). These requests carry no
personal data. Hugging Face, like any web server, sees the IP address a request
comes from; its own privacy policy covers that.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data at all, it collects
no data from children either.

Permissions

Kanji Trainer asks for no special Windows permissions beyond reading and
writing its own data, network access for pack downloads, plus access to a file
you pick yourself when you export or import reports.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with a new release of the app
and the date at the top is updated.
